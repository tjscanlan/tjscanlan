I evaluate AI agents for a living, so I turned my own profile into an eval run.

I'm a Senior AI Engineer building and shipping LLM-powered agents in production — then building the evaluation frameworks (EvalOps) that prove they still work after the tenth change to a prompt, tool, or model. The other half of the job is platform engineering: Kubernetes, Terraform, GCP and AWS, the plumbing that makes agentic features reliable enough to trust.

Before agents, I spent two and a half years as a cybersecurity data engineer, building the ETL pipelines and SQL models that fed the ML and analytics platforms I now help evaluate. I studied math and data science at Merrimack College, which is probably why "did it actually work" is the question I keep coming back to.
